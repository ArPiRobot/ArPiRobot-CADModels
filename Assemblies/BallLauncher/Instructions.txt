Launcher Assuemby: 
  - Print each component (other than the motor) separately and assemble)
  - Screws used to attach rotating components. Machine screw / bolt style threading with nut

ExitAssembly:
  - Print the entire assembly in one piece. Orient with arms guiding ball upwards. Tree supports

MountAssembly:
  - Print the entire assembly in one piece.

HolsterTube:
  - Single part. Print and glue to exit assembly with superglue

Ball:
  - Use superglue and guide peg to glue halves together
  - Print multiple to load into holseter tube

Required screws:
  - Threaded (wood screw style) to attach launcher assembly to motor. Recessed into rotating arm part
  - Bolts to attach mount assembly to the motor
